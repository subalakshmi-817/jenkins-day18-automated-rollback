========================================================
OBJECTIVE
========================================================

The objective of Day 18 is to understand automated rollback.

Rollback means returning the application to a previous stable
version when a newly deployed version fails.

========================================================
JENKINS CONCEPT
========================================================

Jenkins automates the deployment process.

Instead of manually executing every deployment command,
Jenkins executes the commands through the Jenkins Pipeline.

========================================================
ROLLBACK CONCEPT
========================================================

If the new version passes health checks:

New Version = SUCCESS

If the new version fails health checks:

New Version = FAILURE

The stable version should be restored.
